What We Do

Specialist services for the oilfield

Every service we offer is built around one industry. From full rig relocations to single-load equipment moves, we handle cargo that ordinary hauliers can't.

Rig Moves & Relocation

Complete rig-down, transport, and rig-up moves for land drilling rigs and workover units — planned load-by-load and executed with precision scheduling.

Heavy & Oversized Haulage

Low-bed, extendable, and multi-axle transport for out-of-gauge oilfield loads — mud pumps, draw-works, substructures, generators, and camp units.

Drilling Equipment Transport

Dedicated movement of drilling packages, BOP stacks, top drives, pipe-handling systems, and wellhead equipment between yards, bases, and well sites.

Pipes, Casing & OCTG

Bulk haulage of line pipe, casing, tubing, and drill pipe with correct stowage, strapping, and handling to protect threads and coatings in transit.

Cranage & Lifting Support

Crane coordination, rigging, and lifting supervision for loading and offloading heavy oilfield cargo — one team from hook-on to set-down.

Route Surveys, Permits & Escorts

Pre-move route studies, load permits, community liaison, and escort vehicles — the groundwork that keeps abnormal loads legal and moving.

Sea & Land Coordination

Integrated movement across jetties, flow stations, and swamp locations — coordinating barges and trucking so cargo transitions without delay.

Oilfield Equipment Logistics

End-to-end movement planning for production, processing, and support equipment — strictly for the oil and gas sector, nothing else.

Who We Are

Built for one industry: oil & gas

Sealand Haulage Limited is a logistics company that serves the oil and gas industry exclusively. We do not spread ourselves across general freight — our people, equipment, and procedures are dedicated to moving rigs and the heavy, high-value assets that keep the oilfield running.

Rig moves are our core business. We understand that a rig standing idle is money lost, so every relocation is planned in detail before the first load rolls: load lists, route surveys, lift plans, and contingency measures are in place ahead of mobilisation.

From the highway to the jetty, from open yards to remote well sites, we combine land haulage and marine coordination — the "sea" and "land" in our name — to give operators and drilling contractors one accountable partner for the whole move.

  • Oil & gas onlyA specialist fleet and team focused on one sector's cargo and standards.
  • Rig-move expertiseSequenced, load-by-load relocations that minimise rig downtime.
  • One accountable partnerTrucking, lifting, permits, and marine legs coordinated under one roof.
  • Compliance readyDocumentation and journey management aligned with operator HSE requirements.
Safety & Compliance

HSE is how we operate, not a checkbox

Heavy loads and live oilfield environments leave no room for shortcuts. Our operations are run to the safety standards the industry demands.

Journey Management

Planned routes, driver rest rules, convoy communication, and live trip monitoring on every movement.

Certified Lifting

Colour-coded, inspected rigging and competent banksmen on all lifting operations.

Trained Personnel

Drivers and crews inducted to operator HSE standards, with toolbox talks before every critical task.

Maintained Fleet

Scheduled inspection and maintenance of trucks, trailers, and support equipment — no defective unit goes on the road.
